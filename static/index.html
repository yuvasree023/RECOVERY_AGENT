The 7 Safety Guardrails (`guardrails.py`)

Rule 1
Opt-Out Protection

customer.opt_out_status == True → Immediate TERMINATE, 0 contacts dispatched.

Rule 2
Fraud Risk Threshold

event.fraud_score > 0.8 → Immediate TERMINATE & flags for manual review (EVT_1005 test).

Rule 3
Contact Attempt Cap

case.current_attempt >= max_attempts (3) → TERMINATE.

Rule 4
Reasoning Loop Circuit Breaker

case.loop_iterations >= max_loop_iterations (4) → ESCALATE to human desk.

Rule 5
Contact Cooldown Enforcement

No two ACT calls within retry_cooldown_hours for the same case.

Rule 6
DND Regulation Compliance

Prohibits WhatsApp & SMS dispatches during DND window (21:00 - 08:00).

Rule 7
Control Group Isolation

Cases in the 20% control group bypass all ACT calls to measure true incremental lift.
