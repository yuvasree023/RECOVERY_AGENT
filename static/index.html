  • Rule 1 (Opt-Out): Halts communication immediately if customer has opted out.

  • Rule 3 (Max Attempts): Caps total recovery contacts to 3 attempts.
  • Rule 4 (Circuit Breaker): Escalates to human desk after 5 loop iterations.
  • Rule 5 (Cooldown Interval): Enforces 2-hour quiet interval between retries.
  • Rule 6 (Telecom DND): Curfew prevents SMS/WhatsApp between 21:00 and 08:00 (triggers replan to 24/7 Email).
  • Rule 7 (Control Isolation): Isolates 20% holdout group for unbiased baseline lift measurement.
  • Rule 8 (WhatsApp Consent): Verifies explicit opt-in before using WhatsApp.
  • Rule 9 (Margin Cap): Strictly bounds discount incentives to ≤ 20.0%.
