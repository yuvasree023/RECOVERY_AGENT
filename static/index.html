1. Payment Failure Recovery

Live Playbook

Handles involuntary churn from credit card, debit card, and UPI payment decline codes.

  • Insufficient Funds: 2h cooldown before retry; respectful reminder link via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Card Expired: Automated retries halted; customer prompted with secure card update portal.
  • Network & Bank Timeout: Rapid automated retry scheduled post-cooldown.
  • Card Declined: Alternate payment method offered (UPI intent / QR).

2. Checkout Drop-off Recovery

Live Playbook

Recovers high-intent shopping carts abandoned during payment selection or shipping calculation.

  • Initial Abandonment: Passive cart summary dispatched via preferred channel.
  • High Shipping Cost Friction: AI offers bounded discount incentive (strictly capped at 10%).
  • Multi-Turn Adaptation: Observes item inventory and cart expiry windows.
  • Margin Protection: High-tier carts receive assistance without unnecessary discounts.

3. Subscription Mandate Recovery

Live Playbook

Automated handling for recurring e-mandates (RBI e-mandate guidelines, UPI AutoPay, credit cards).

  • Pre-debit Notification: Sent 24h prior to mandate charge.
  • Failed Mandate Retry: Delayed execution matching salary cycles (1st to 5th of month).
  • Fallback Payment Link: One-time link sent if auto-debit fails after 2 attempts.
  • Grace Period Management: Keeps service active while dunning workflow runs.

4. B2B Receivables Recovery

Configured Playbook

Commercial dunning workflow for past-due enterprise invoices and net-30 accounts.

  • Invoice Reminder: Formal statement sent on Day 1 past due.
  • Dispute Routing: If customer flags billing error, routes to human accounts receivable desk.
  • Promise to Pay (PTP): Records agreed payment date and pauses automated notices.
  • Credit Hold Warning: Compliant warning before restricting service access.
